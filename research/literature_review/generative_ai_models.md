# Literature Review: Generative AI Models in Art

## Abstract
A review of recent advancements in text-to-image synthesis models, comparing diffusion models, GANs, and transformer-based architectures.

## Key Papers
1. High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models (Rombach et al., 2022)
2. DALL-E 2: Hierarchical Text-Conditional Image Generation (Ramesh et al., 2022)
3. Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models (Google, 2023)
